# Sources

Every number on the page and the slides comes from these sources. Each research thread in `analysis/<thread>/manifest.json` lists every file downloaded, with its link, the date it was accessed and its licence. No number comes from a news story; news reports were used only to find official documents.

## Methods borrowed from published research

- Canadian Suburbs project (David Gordon, Queen's University), methods: active core, transit suburb, auto suburb and exurban. https://www.canadiansuburbs.ca/methods/
- Kitschelt, H. and P. Rehm (2023), "Polarity Reversal: The Socioeconomic Reconfiguration of Partisan Support in Knowledge Societies", Politics and Society 51(4): 520 to 566. https://doi.org/10.1177/00323292221100220
- Goodman, L. A. (1953), "Ecological Regressions and Behavior of Individuals", American Sociological Review 18(6).
- Gallagher, M., Election indices (least squares index of disproportionality), Trinity College Dublin. https://www.tcd.ie/Political_Science/about/people/Michael.Gallagher/ElSystems/Docts/ElectionIndices.pdf
- Laakso, M. and R. Taagepera (1979), "Effective" Number of Parties, Comparative Political Studies 12(1).
- Pedersen, M. N. (1979), "The Dynamics of European Party Systems: Changing Patterns of Electoral Volatility", European Journal of Political Research 7(1).
