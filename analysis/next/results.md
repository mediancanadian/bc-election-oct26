### 1d. Total cost and how it is paid for

The platform gives no single "total cost" figure. Its costing (Appendices A and B) states:

- New operating spending: "A Plan for a Stronger BC adds new operating spending of est. $1.425 billion a year at full cost" (p. 60). Phased in (Table B1, p. 65): $173M in 2027/28, $658M in 2028/29, $1,080M in 2029/30; full cost reached in 2030/31. Envelopes: Affordable BC $150M, Prosperous BC $72M, Healthy BC $975M, Safer BC $223M, Strong and Proud BC $5M (pp. 61 to 63).
- Tax relief (Table B2, p. 66): "Total tax relief" $725M (2026/27), $3,400M, $3,475M, $3,475M; "Affordability measures" (adds PST deferral on major projects) $725M, $3,480M, $3,555M, $3,555M. Text: "British Columbians get $11.1 billion in tax relief" (p. 68).
- New capital: "the aircraft fleet and planning for the Surrey women's and children's hospital" ... "comes to about $425 million" (p. 58).
- How it is paid for:
  - Growth: "the province collects $2.62 billion more over three years than the NDP's own forecast expects" ... "without raising a single tax" (p. 54); assumes real GDP growth of "2.5 per cent in 2027, 2.8 per cent in 2028 and 3.2 per cent in 2029" (p. 54). Table: "Net new revenue from growth 296 857 1,465 2,618" ($M, 2027/28 to 2029/30, three years) (p. 55).
  - Spending restraint (Table B3, p. 67): "Total spending restraint 1,050 3,020 3,599 4,068 11,737" ($M, 2026/27 to 2029/30, four years). Components: contingencies cut ($7,000M over four years: "Cut the Contingencies Vote from $5 billion to $4 billion to $3 billion", p. 66), "Freeze hiring outside the front line" ($2,009M), "Core review of ministry spending" ($1,853M), "Cut outside consultants and contracts" ($875M).
  - Capital: "We will find the room within the capital plan through the audit, competitive bidding and a bridge that costs less than the tunnel" (p. 58); Massey bridge built "within the money already set aside for the crossing" and "ask Ottawa to apply its announced contribution of up to $3 billion to the bridge" (p. 57).
- Deficit path (Table B4, p. 70): "Conservative deficit 13,689 13,536 12,303 10,799" ($M, 2026/27 to 2029/30). Text: "It then falls every year ... to $10.8 billion in 2029/30" (p. 68).
- Balance: no date. "We will chart a realistic return to balance over time with spending benchmarks and guardrails as we grow the economy." (p. 9). Four "fiscal anchors" from the first budget (p. 59).
- Process commitments with timelines: independent fiscal panel to "report publicly before our first budget" (p. 55); capital plan audit to "report out within six months" (p. 10); tax reform panel "to report within the first 180 days" (p. 14); "Cut red tape back to 2017 levels by the end of the first term" (p. 13).
